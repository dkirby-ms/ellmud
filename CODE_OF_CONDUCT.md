# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in the Ellmud community — whether in issues, discussions, pull requests, or elsewhere — will be treated with respect and have their contributions valued.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassing or discriminatory comments related to gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, mental illness, neuroatypicality, physical appearance, body, age, race, or religion
- Insulting, demeaning, or unwelcoming comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

If someone engages in behavior that violates this code of conduct:

1. **First offense:** A maintainer will address it privately and clearly explain the violation.
2. **Repeated violations:** The person may be warned or temporarily muted.
3. **Serious violations:** Immediate ban from the community.

To report a violation, contact the maintainers directly via private message or email.

## Scope

This code applies to all spaces associated with the Ellmud community, including GitHub (issues, discussions, PRs), Discord, and other communication channels.
