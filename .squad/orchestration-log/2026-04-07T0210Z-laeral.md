## Assignment

Design visual system for grid combat creatures and environments, focusing on:
- Creature silhouettes and threat visualization
- Faction-based color palettes and atmosphere
- Player representation and team identification
- Art direction and 32×32px pixel art constraints
- Design principles ensuring text-first gameplay

## Deliverables Produced

**File:** `docs/design/337-combat-grid-visual-design.md`

- Creature categories (Humanoid, Bestial, Swarming, Amorphous, Drone) with shape language
- Color-to-threat mapping (saturation/vibrancy = danger level)
- Size scaling (1×1 common, 2×2+ bosses) for visual weight
- Disposition badges (hostile, alerted, neutral) and state overlays (wounds, downed)
- Faction color palettes (Kindari grey/rust, Bloom green/cyan, Krewe purple/gold)
- Terrain tile layers (base + hazard overlays)
- Fog of war implementation (partial visibility, ghosted off-screen)
- Player sprites with visible equipment and PvP threat indicators
- Art direction: 32×32px pixel art, limited palettes (24-32 colors), minimal animation (2-4 frames)
- Reference styles: DCSS (readability) + Cogmind (sci-fi) + Caves of Qud (mutation)

**Inbox Entry:** `.squad/decisions/inbox/laeral-combat-grid.md`

- Decision: Grid combat as optional, text-first supplementary overlay
- Enforcement of text-primary identity (grid updates sync with text, never ahead)
- Technical implications for each team member (Minsc, Regis, Bruenor, Drizzt)
- Deferred decisions (animated terrain, destructible terrain, grid-only abilities)
