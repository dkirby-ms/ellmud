## Key Design Decisions

1. **Round-robin distribution** — Fair, deterministic, no randomness
2. **Same-room only** — Prevents AFK members in safe zones from receiving loot
3. **Weight capacity checks** — Respects player carry limits, members effectively "refuse" items if over-encumbered
4. **Default OFF** — Groups must explicitly enable; prevents unintended distribution
5. **Leader-only toggle** — Prevents griefing and disputes
6. **Items only** — No XP or currency in this phase; scope tight for future expansion
