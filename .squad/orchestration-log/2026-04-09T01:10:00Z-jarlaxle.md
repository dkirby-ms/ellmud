## Design Decisions Applied
- **JSONB config blob** over schema per-setting (avoids future migration churn)
- **Server-side validation** (not client-determined) for gameplay settings
- **No middleware auth** (per existing pattern)
- **Default on GET** (no unnecessary DB writes)
- **Scope boundaries:** No keybind export, profiles, or .rcfile upload in v1
